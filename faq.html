FAQ

Common questions about working with iCode Solutions.

What kinds of projects do you build?

We build websites, web applications, mobile apps, enterprise software, integrations, cloud solutions and automation platforms.

Can you work with an existing application?

Yes. We can review, redesign, modernize, integrate or extend an existing product.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes. Support can include maintenance, monitoring, bug fixes, performance improvements and feature development.

How do projects start?

Most engagements begin with a short discovery phase to understand the problem, users, scope, risks and expected outcomes.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes, where required and mutually agreed before sensitive project details are shared.

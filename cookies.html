Cookie Policy

Information about cookies that may be used on the iCode Solutions website.

Effective date: August 21, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored by a browser to help websites remember information and understand usage.

Strictly necessary cookies

These support core website functions and generally cannot be switched off where they are essential to operation.

Analytics cookies

Analytics cookies can help measure traffic and usage patterns. Only describe and enable these if the production site actually uses an analytics provider.

Preference cookies

These may remember user choices such as language, display settings or consent preferences.

Marketing cookies

Marketing cookies should only be used where applicable and with any consent required by law.

Managing cookies

Visitors can manage cookies through browser settings and, where implemented, through the website's cookie preference controls.
